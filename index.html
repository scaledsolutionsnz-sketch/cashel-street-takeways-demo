Our shop

A proper Christchurch chippy, still doing it by hand

Cashel Street Takeways sits on the Linwood end of Cashel Street, a few minutes out of the central city. It's a takeaway shop, not a restaurant — you ring, you walk in, you get a hot parcel and you're away.

What's kept people coming is boring and unglamorous: the fish gets filleted here, the oil gets looked after, and nothing goes in the fryer until someone's ordered it. That's the whole trick.

  • Fish filleted on site, not bought in pre-cut
  • Clean oil and a drained, non-greasy feed
  • Rolls and savouries made in the shop, not out of a box
