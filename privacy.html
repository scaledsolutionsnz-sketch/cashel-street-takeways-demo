Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Cashel Street Takeways, 341 Cashel Street, Linwood, Christchurch 8011, New Zealand. Phone 03 261 9333. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. There are no forms on this site

This website doesn't collect any information from you directly. There is no contact form, no booking form and no feedback form anywhere on it. The only ways to reach us are the phone number and the email button, and both of those open in your own phone or email app.

3. What we collect when you get in touch

  • Phone orders: your name and, sometimes, a contact number so we can call you if there's a problem with the order.
  • Email: whatever you choose to put in your message, along with your email address.
  • In the shop: nothing beyond what you tell us at the counter.

We use that information for one thing: getting your order right and answering your question. We don't sell it, rent it, or add you to a mailing list.

4. Payments

Payments are taken in the shop through our EFTPOS terminal or in cash. Card details are handled by the payment provider and their bank. We don't see, store or process your card number.

5. Information collected automatically

Our website host records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit, for security and to keep the site running. This isn't used to identify you personally.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Maps. The map at the bottom of the home page is embedded from Google, and loading it means your browser talks to Google's servers under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google's font service.
  • Google Business Profile. Reviews shown on this site are public reviews left on our Google listing.
  • Facebook, Instagram, TikTok. Links out to those platforms. Once you're there, their policies apply, not ours.

7. Cookies

This site doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Embedded services like Google Maps may set their own cookies in your browser. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

8. How long we keep things

Phone order details are kept only as long as needed to fill the order. Emails are kept while they're useful for answering you and for our own records, then deleted.

9. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Ring the shop or email us and we'll sort it out.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Ring 03 261 9333 or email us.
